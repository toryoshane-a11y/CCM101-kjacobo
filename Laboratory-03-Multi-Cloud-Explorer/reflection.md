# Mission Reflection

After completing this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was AWS. 
I think AWS has a lot of services and tools that can be used for different types of 
businesses. It also seems useful for both small companies and large organizations.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide virtual machines, storage, 
databases, identity management, and Kubernetes services. They also offer tools for 
security, networking, and application development. Even though the services may have 
different names, they are designed to solve similar problems.

Business requirements can have a big effect on which cloud platform a company should 
choose. For example, a company that already uses many Microsoft products may benefit 
more from Azure. A company that needs strong AI and machine learning tools might choose 
GCP. A startup might choose AWS because of its wide range of services and flexibility. 
This shows that there is no single cloud provider that is best for every business.

As a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned that it is important to understand the 
customer's needs before recommending a platform. Factors such as cost, security, 
performance, existing technology, and future growth should all be considered. The 
recommendation should be based on what is best for the business rather than just 
choosing the platform I personally prefer.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved after completing this mission because 
I now have more examples of my cloud knowledge and decision-making. The comparison 
tables and recommendations show that I can compare cloud services and explain why a 
certain platform may be better for a specific situation. Overall, this mission helped 
me understand cloud computing from a more practical and business-focused perspective.
